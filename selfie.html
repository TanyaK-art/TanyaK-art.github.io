Tanya Kruse

I took inspiration from Seth Tobek for my self portrait. I struggled with all the different coordinates for the variety of shapes like ellipses and triangles, squares, etc... and really liked how Seth's hair came out so I set out to rebuild my self portrait with a reference photo and image mapping so I could figure out the coordinates for the vertices. I hate doing self portraits. A year ago, in my oil painting class, I restarted and used 3 different canvases, even re doing my ground painting and starting over several times on one canvas, and think I cried 3 different times trying to do that self portrait. Ironically enough, I think I cried 3 times trying to do this one. I am happy with the results of this one. I did a couple hours of work for at least 4 days to get this outcome.
